# Design contract
Brand direction: instrument-grade editorial technology retail. Refined product catalogue, clear numerals and technical details, sharp hierarchy; avoid neon gamer visuals, arbitrary gradients, card soup and fake reviews.
Colors: ink #151717, paper #f6f7f5, muted #69706e, white #ffffff, signal accent #ccff5f, borders #e5e8e4.
Typography: system sans, strong weight hierarchy, precise tracking. Desktop max 1240px, responsive density, 44px touch targets and focus-visible rings.
Product imagery during this phase uses stylized abstract devices, never misrepresented actual photos. Everything demo identified.
